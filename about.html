About TnD Adventures Ltd

A Kampala-based tour operator built on straightforward planning, local knowledge, and guides who know Uganda's parks well.

Our Story

Built by people who know these roads

TnD Adventures Ltd was started by a small team of Uganda-based guides and planners who were tired of seeing travelers pay high agency mark-ups for trips that could be arranged more simply and more personally. We work directly with drivers, ranger stations, lodges, and community guides across the country, which keeps our pricing honest and our planning accurate.

Today we plan safaris, gorilla treks, and custom tours for independent travelers, families, and groups from across the world, with every itinerary shaped around the traveler rather than a fixed package.

How We Work

Direct planning, clear pricing

When you contact us, we ask about your dates, budget, and interests, then propose an itinerary with transport, accommodation, park fees, and guiding costs laid out clearly. There are no hidden charges added later.

Once a trip is confirmed, one of our guides stays in contact with you from arrival to departure, so you always have a direct line to someone on the ground in Uganda.

Our Historical Ventures

How we got here

A short trail of milestones behind the "10+ years guiding travelers" on our homepage.

2015

First guided trips, Lake Mburo

Started out running small group trips to Lake Mburo and Queen Elizabeth for friends and referrals, using hired vehicles and borrowed contacts at the park gates.

2016

Murchison Falls added to the route

Built a direct relationship with boat operators and rangers at Murchison Falls, adding it as a regular stop on our northern itineraries.

2018

TnD Adventures Ltd registered

Formalized the business in Kampala, moved to our own fleet of vehicles, and brought on our first full-time field guides.

2019

Kidepo Valley expeditions launched

Extended our reach into Karamoja, running multi-day trips to Uganda's most remote park for travelers who wanted to go further off the usual route.

2022

Gorilla and chimp permits, direct booking

Began booking gorilla and chimpanzee trekking permits directly with the Uganda Wildlife Authority, cutting out permit resellers for our clients.

Today

400+ trips across 8 national parks

Now a small, hands-on team covering 8 of Uganda's national parks, still built around direct pricing and guides who know the ground.
